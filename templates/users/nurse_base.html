Nurse/Caretaker Guidelines

Your Core Workflow
  • Step 1: Update your Availability so families know when you're free. (Note: The system securely monitors your calendar to prevent double-booking if you are already engaged in a Full-Time or Live-In assignment).
  • Step 2: Families can now Direct-Book you for your open dates, or you can use Browse Requests to find jobs yourself!
  • Step 3: Once assigned, visit My Patients to log attendance, check daily schedules, and fill daily care reports.
  • Step 4 (Support): Use Chat Support in the sidebar to contact the admin directly for any platform-related help.
Proof of Work & Tasks
  • When completing a task, thoroughly document your actions and provide Proof of Work (e.g., photo of medicine layout, sealed packages).
  • Always submit the Daily Report before logging out to ensure families stay informed.
Privacy & Compliance Warning
  • HIPAA & Privacy Protocol: DO NOT upload or send inappropriate, non-clinical, or overly graphic images of the patient. Keep all proof focused on the *action* (e.g., the food prepared), not the person's privacy.
  • Maintain professional communication in the messaging portal at all times.
  • Any submission of wrong or offensive pictures/messages will lead to immediate account termination and potential legal review.
