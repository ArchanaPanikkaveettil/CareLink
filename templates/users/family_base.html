Family Orientation & Guidelines

Your Core Workflow
  • Step 1: Go to Post Request to create a care listing. Describe your clinical/home needs clearly.
  • Step 2 (Direct Booking): You can bypass public posting! Go to Find Caretakers, browse profiles, and click slots on their calendar to Direct Book them securely to your Open Request.
  • Step 3: Check My Requests or My Bookings to evaluate incoming caretaker applications or direct booking status.
  • Step 4: Once assigned, visit My Caregivers to set up daily care schedules, monitor reports, and verify tasks.
  • Step 5 (Support): Use Chat Support in the sidebar to communicate directly with the admin for any technical issues or assistance.
Task Accountability
  • Always provide clear guidelines for "Due Time" and specific instructions to prevent care errors.
  • Caretakers will upload Proof of Work. Review this imagery before clicking "Verify Care".
Security & Communication Warning
  • Never ask caretakers to transmit inappropriate, overly graphic, or highly sensitive bodily photos via the messaging portal.
  • Do not engage in unverified external transactions. Manage all care directions internally.
  • Any abusive language or protocol violations will result in permanent account suspension.
